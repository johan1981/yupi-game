## Movement information captured

This video is approved as reference for:

- being set down / recovering balance;
- starting from near standstill;
- short acceleration and sprint bursts;

- braking / decelerating;
- jumping or stepping onto a soft raised surface;
- landing / catching the front body;
- low playful movement around a toy / another dog.

### Three-legged biomechanics

- Yupi's **right foreleg** is the single front support.
- During front-body catch/contact the shoulder and chest compress downward briefly.
- The front body then rebounds upward instead of gliding at a constant height.
- Both hind legs deliver much of the propulsion.
- Head and neck visibly participate in the vertical rhythm.
- The tail is actively used for balance during fast turns and direction changes.
- Fast starts/stops contain a short, readable weight transfer.
- Do not animate Yupi as a normal four-legged dog with one leg simply removed.
